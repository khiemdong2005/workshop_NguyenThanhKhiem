### Internship Summary

I took part in the **Workforce Bootcamp - First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** at Amazon Web Services Vietnam from **September 14 to November 28, 2026**. Over 11 weeks, I learned AWS fundamentals through hands-on labs. I became more comfortable reading technical documentation, using the AWS Console and AWS CLI, and checking my results after each lab.

I completed **three technical blog posts** and attended **six program events**. Writing the posts helped me practice explaining technical concepts in my own words. The events gave me a chance to hear how others ask questions and approach problems.

I study Artificial Intelligence at FPT University. During the internship, I spent more time on topics I had less opportunity to practice at university, such as IAM permissions and tracking AWS costs. When I came across a new concept, I usually read the documentation first and then tried it in a lab to see where it was useful.

In the labs, I practiced creating a VPC, learned how public and private subnets differ, and got familiar with storing data in Amazon S3. While studying RAG, I learned to assess document retrieval and answer quality separately.

The FCAJ attendance portal records **10 completed office visits**.

### Personal Reflection

Looking back, I became more proactive about finding documentation, trying again when I got stuck, and recording what I learned. I also became more comfortable discussing my work, listening to feedback, and adjusting my approach.

One change I noticed was how I handled unexpected lab results. I learned to compare my configuration with the documentation and check each step to see where I was getting stuck. When I needed feedback, I could explain more clearly what I had tried and what happened.

I still need to ask questions sooner when requirements are unclear and balance my time more evenly between studying, practicing, and updating my worklog. Those are two habits I want to improve.

### Needs Improvement

* **Be more decisive with ideas and planning:** Commit to clear directions and finalize work plans earlier instead of spending too long hesitating between options.
* **Speak up when unsure and connect proactively with mentors:** Share opinions more boldly, ask questions as soon as doubts come up, and reach out proactively to mentors for timely guidance.
* **Manage and allocate time more effectively:** Balance weekly time more evenly across learning new concepts, building the project, and updating documentation to avoid last-minute crunches.
* **Deepen practical Cloud & AI skills:** Keep strengthening deployment and end-to-end testing skills on AWS so I can handle larger-scale workloads with more confidence.
