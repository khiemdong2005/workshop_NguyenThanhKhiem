### Internship Summary

During my internship as a **Workforce Bootcamp - First Cloud AI Journey** trainee at **Amazon Web Services Viet Nam Company Limited** from **14/09/2026** to **28/11/2026**, I had the opportunity to bridge my **Artificial Intelligence** background at **FPT University** (class cbt_team) with practical experience in designing, building, and operating AI applications on AWS cloud infrastructure.

Across the 11 weeks of the program, I started with AWS fundamentals—account security (IAM, MFA, AWS Budgets), multi-tier Amazon VPC networking, storage services (S3, DynamoDB, Data Lake), and high-availability concepts—before applying them directly to the **MediMind Clinical AI** project. Throughout the project, I contributed from problem scoping and drafting the project proposal ([Section 2](../2-proposal/)) to developing core modules such as symptom triage, refining the prescription OCR pipeline (resolving misread drug names by pairing full-prescription images with cropped text regions), RAG search, bilingual nutrition, appointment booking/notifications, and documenting live AWS deployment in ap-southeast-1 in the Workshop ([Section 5](../5-workshop/), covering a 3-tier Multi-AZ VPC, private RDS PostgreSQL, Secrets Manager, ECR/CodeBuild, EC2 + EBS, SSM Session Manager, 2/2 Healthy ALB targets, AWS WAF, CloudFront, EFS, Auto Scaling Group, CloudWatch, and AWS Budgets).

Alongside project work and hands-on labs, I completed **3 technical blog posts** exploring AWS healthcare AI and RAG evaluation services (**Amazon Comprehend Medical**, **Amazon Transcribe Medical**, and **Amazon Bedrock Evaluations**) and attended **6 community events** under the First Cloud AI Journey program. Through these activities, I strengthened my skills in AI integration, debugging from real-world failure cases, using the AWS CLI/Console, and writing bilingual technical documentation.

In terms of work ethic, I maintained a proactive learning attitude, took responsibility for assigned tasks, welcomed feedback to resolve issues, and collaborated with the team to deliver the final report.
