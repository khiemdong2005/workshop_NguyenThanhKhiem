These three posts cover clinical-data processing and RAG evaluation on AWS. Each page summarizes the published topic and links to the AWS documentation used to verify the technical details.

### [Blog 1 – Amazon Comprehend Medical: Extracting data from clinical text](3.1-Blog1/)

This post explains clinical entity detection, PHI detection, negation, and terminology linking to ICD-10-CM, RxNorm, and SNOMED CT. It also discusses language limits and human review.

### [Blog 2 – Amazon Transcribe Medical: Turning medical speech into text](3.2-Blog2/)

This post covers medical dictation and conversation transcription, speaker labeling, custom vocabulary, and the review needed before a transcript is used in patient care.

### [Blog 3 – Amazon Bedrock Evaluations: Assessing RAG applications](3.3-Blog3/)

This post distinguishes retrieval evaluation from retrieval-and-generation evaluation, explains metrics such as Context relevance and Faithfulness, and shows how to interpret results when improving a RAG system.
